Privacy Policy

Meds For Less — www.medsforless.net

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Meds For Less ("we", "us", "our") operates the website www.medsforless.net and communicates with customers via WhatsApp Business, email, and other messaging platforms. We are an online platform that facilitates the procurement and delivery of affordable prescription medicines from licensed Indian pharmacy partners to customers worldwide, with a primary focus on the UAE and surrounding region.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our website, WhatsApp messaging service, or any other communication channel to interact with us. By using our services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to process your medicine orders and provide our services:

  • Name and contact information (phone number, email address)
  • Delivery address
  • Prescription details and medicine requirements shared by you
  • Photos of prescriptions or medicine packaging sent via WhatsApp or other channels
  • Payment transaction records
  • Communication history (WhatsApp messages, emails) related to your orders
  • Website usage data (cookies, IP address, browser type) collected automatically

We do not collect sensitive personal information beyond what is necessary to fulfil your medicine order. We do not request or store financial account numbers, credit card details, or government ID numbers unless specifically required for customs documentation at your request.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected for the following purposes:

  • Processing and fulfilling your medicine orders
  • Communicating with you about your orders, quotes, and delivery status
  • Verifying prescriptions with our pharmacy partners
  • Providing customer support via WhatsApp, email, or other channels
  • Sending order confirmations, delivery updates, and follow-up communications
  • Improving our services and website functionality
  • Complying with applicable laws and regulations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. WhatsApp Business Communication

We use the WhatsApp Business Platform (powered by Meta) to communicate with customers. When you message us on WhatsApp:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name are received by our system
  • Messages, images, and documents you send are processed to fulfil your order
  • We may use automated systems to acknowledge your message and collect initial order details
  • Your conversations are stored securely for order processing and customer service purposes

WhatsApp messages are encrypted in transit by WhatsApp. Our automated systems process messages solely for the purpose of providing our medicine procurement and delivery service. You may opt out of WhatsApp communications at any time by sending "STOP" to our WhatsApp number.

5. Information Sharing

We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With licensed Indian pharmacy partners, solely for the purpose of procuring your prescribed medicines (prescription details only, shared on a need-to-know basis)
  • With courier and logistics providers, solely for the purpose of delivering your order (name and delivery address only)
  • With payment processors, solely for the purpose of processing your payment
  • When required by law, regulation, or legal process

We do not share your medical information, prescription details, or personal health data with any third party other than the pharmacy partner fulfilling your specific order.

6. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information:

  • Order data is stored securely with access limited to authorised personnel
  • WhatsApp communications are encrypted in transit
  • We do not store prescription images longer than necessary to fulfil your order
  • Payment information is processed through secure payment gateways and is not stored on our systems

While we implement appropriate safeguards, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Order records and communication history are retained for a reasonable period to support customer service, reorders, and any regulatory requirements. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of WhatsApp communications by sending "STOP"
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details provided below.

9. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website (www.medsforless.net) may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience, analyse website traffic, and understand how visitors interact with our site. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Medicine orders must be placed by adults with valid prescriptions from registered medical practitioners.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Your continued use of our services after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
